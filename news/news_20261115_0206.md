Its early stages often pass without noticeable symptoms, leaving many people unaware that they need medical attention.As World Diabetes Day is observed on November 14, the importance of early detection deserves attention. A simple health check can uncover raised blood sugar levels, identify prediabetes and help people make changes before diabetes develops or complications begin. According to Dr Vikram Vora, Medical Director, Indian Subcontinent, International SOS, early detection is particularly important because Type 2 diabetes can progress silently.
