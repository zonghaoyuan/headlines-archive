Recent theories have predicted quantum criticality endowed with nontrivial topology mostly in one-dimensional (1D) systems3–5, pointing to a potential unification of the two patterns. Yet experimental observation of such critical topology, that is, topology at phase boundaries and its extension to higher dimensions, remain outstanding. Our work demonstrates a fundamental connection between topology and criticality, paving the way for an experimental route towards exploring topological physics at phase transitions.
